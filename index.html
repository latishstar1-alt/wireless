Wi-Fi Transmission Using Optics

Explore how digital data can be transmitted wirelessly using modulated light.

Scientific note — conventional Wi-Fi (IEEE 802.11) transmits over radio frequencies. This lab simulates optical wireless communication — commonly called Li-Fi — where an LED rapidly changes its optical intensity to encode the same kind of digital data, using light instead of radio waves as the carrier.

04Modulation

Different modulation schemes encode bits by changing a property of the optical signal. OOK is the simplest to see: 1 = LED ON, 0 = LED OFF.

OOK simply switches the LED fully on for a 1 and off for a 0 — easy to visualize and implement. ASK instead varies the LED's brightness between two non-zero levels. PWM keeps the LED fully on/off but changes the fraction of time it stays on within each bit slot (duty cycle).

05Optical transmitter

An LED can switch its optical intensity extremely rapidly — far faster than the human eye can perceive — allowing digital information to be encoded directly into the emitted light.
LED OPTICAL POWER80%

Electrical Signal → LED Driver → LED. Human eyes cannot perceive the extremely rapid intensity changes used for this kind of communication.

06Free-space optical channel & ambient noise

Light travels from the LED, through open space, to the photodetector. Distance, ambient light, alignment and attenuation all shape what actually arrives.

DISTANCE 3.5 m
AMBIENT LIGHT 20%

Optical power spreads out and weakens the farther it travels — increasing distance reduces the power that reaches the photodetector.
Ambient optical sources such as sunlight or room lighting introduce unwanted variations into the photodetector output, making transmitted 1s and 0s harder to distinguish.
The photodetector receives less optical power when the transmitter and receiver are not pointed directly at each other.

10What does a modem actually do?

A modem performs signal conversion — modulation and demodulation — so information can travel across a physical channel. Tap to expand. ▾

Transmitter modem

Digital data → modulated electrical signal → optical transmitter. It converts bits into a form the LED can broadcast as light.

Receiver modem

Photodetector signal → threshold demodulation → digital data. It converts detected light variations back into the original bits.

11Radio Wi-Fi vs. optical wireless communication

Both transmit information wirelessly — they simply use different physical carriers. This lab demonstrates the optical (Li-Fi) approach, not a modification of standard radio Wi-Fi.
